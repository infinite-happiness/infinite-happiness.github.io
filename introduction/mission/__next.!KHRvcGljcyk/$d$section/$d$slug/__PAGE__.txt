# [Ánanda Márga](#term:AnandaMarga) Meaning
[Ánanda Márga](#term:AnandaMarga) means *the path of bliss*.
Ánanda is defined as bliss, and further explained as *infinite happiness*.
The path of bliss is a path where you get to experience true happiness by getting to know, and live by, your true nature — [dharma](/introduction/dharma).

# Motto
The mission, as well as motto, of [Ánanda Márga](#term:AnandaMarga) is:
> [átmamokśárthaḿ Jagat hitáya ca](#term:atmamoksarthamJagaddhitayaCa)
>
> – Self-realization and service to the Universe.

Selfless service is vital for spiritual practice, just as spiritual practice is essential for true social service.

# Self-Realization
The spiritual aspect of the mission is [átmamokśarthaḿ](#term:atmamoksartham), or self-realization.
It means to realize your fullest potential, not just as a human being, but as a divine being.

Self-realization is the ultimate goal of spirituality, which means to get to know yourself on the deepermost level of who you are.

A more literal translation of [átmamokśarthaḿ](#term:atmamoksartham) is the complete and permanent liberation of the soul.
As the soul is liberated from worldly attachments, the desire for God increases.
God comes closer and closer to the practitioner, through more intense spiritual practice, until there is no distinction between the personal self and God.
There is no selfishness or petty ego left.
Only pure divinity, complete consciousness.

Hence, the end goal of [Ánanda Márga](#term:AnandaMarga) is very clear and precise.
The process is also explained in very exact detail.

Self-realization is the goal of [Tantra](#term:Tantra), as well as of [Yoga](#term:Yoga).
For [Ánanda Márga](#term:AnandaMarga), it is also the essence of all teachings, both spiritual and social.

Read more about the spiritual practice on the [Tantra Yoga](/introduction/tantra-yoga) page.


# Service
Selfless service, or [seva](#term:seva), takes many forms.
It encompasses everything from protecting ecosystems to providing all beings with basic needs such as food and healthcare.
For humans, this extends to offering clothing, housing and education, as well as spiritual guidance and training.
Innovation and art are also forms of service, as long as they are guided by welfare.

Historically, [seva](#term:seva) is strongly associated with [Bhakti Yoga](#term:BhaktiYoga), but it also holds an important place in Tantra.

According to [Ánanda Márga](#term:AnandaMarga), [seva](#term:seva) is understood as loving service to God.
It is a vital part of spirituality that should not be neglected.

Most spiritual paths include a philosophy and practice, together with benevolence as a key feature.
Yet, research indicates that the majority seldom engage in hands-on volunteering, despite what their philosophy advocates.
Furthermore, the vast majority of financial donations do not fund charitable work.
Instead, they normally go to running the religious organization itself.

[Ánanda Márga](#term:AnandaMarga) views service as more than an ideal.
[Ánanda Márga](#term:AnandaMarga) envisions actively practiced benevolence as foundational across all aspects of society, including education, arts, science, economy, politics, and different expressions of culture.
Therefore, [Ánanda Márga](#term:AnandaMarga) is often described as socio-spiritual.

[Ánanda Márga Pracáraka Saḿgha](#term:AnandaMargaPracarakaSamgha) certiainly puts service into practice around the world.

# A World Family
[Ánanda Márga](#term:AnandaMarga) does not have a we-against-them point of view.
Instead, we are a global family where every person has equal rights and opportunities to thrive.
Criminals should be given the opportunity to redeem themselves.

# The Organization
[Ánanda Márga Pracáraka Saḿgha](https://www.anandamarga.org) is an international socio-spiritual organization, based on the philosophy and practices of [Ánanda Márga](#term:AnandaMarga).
Globally, the organization runs numerous orphanages, schools and medical clinics, free for those in need.

For decades, the sister organizations [AMURT](https://www.amurt.org) and [AMURTEL](https://amurtel.org) have provided community development, food distribution,
as well as social and mental support, alongside relief for disasters like earthquakes, floods, and refugee crises.

[Ánanda Márga Pracáraka Saḿgha](#term:AnandaMargaPracarakaSamgha) also runs Yoga centers, self-sufficient and sustainable ecovillages, and welfare centers around the world.

At the core of the organizations there are [ácárya](#term:acarya)s and [sanyásii](#term:sanyasii)s,
nuns and monks who serve as meditation teachers and dedicate their lives for the mission of [Ánanda Márga](#term:AnandaMarga).

# Guru
The philosophy and practices of [Ánanda Márga](#term:AnandaMarga) are created by the [Guru](#term:Guru) [Shrii Shrii Ánandamúrti](#term:ShriiShriiAnandamurti)ji (21 May 1921 - 21 October 1990), affectionately called [Bábá](#term:Baba).

Those who met [Bábá](#term:Baba) in person would often describe it as the most powerful experience of their lives.
He would reveal precise details about people's past, knowing things nobody else could possibly know.

When [Bábá](#term:Baba) held discourses, every participant felt they were tailored specifically for them individually.

The depth and breadth of [Ánanda Márga](#term:AnandaMarga) philosophy consists of [Bábá](#term:Baba)'s exceptional knowledge of both the Universe and its history.
There is no speculation or assumption, it stands solely on the word of a true Guru.
